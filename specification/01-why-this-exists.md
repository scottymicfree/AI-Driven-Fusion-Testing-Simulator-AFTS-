# Why This Exists

Scientific progress depends on the ability to test ideas safely, repeatedly, and transparently. As physical systems become more tightly coupled and sensitive, simulation becomes an essential scientific instrument rather than only a convenience.

AFTS proposes an open architectural framework for site-aware, AI-assisted multiphysics simulation. It focuses on fusion and hybrid acoustic-plasma systems, while remaining general enough that implementations may adapt its contracts and validation discipline to other high-sensitivity scientific domains.

The repository exists so the concept may be examined, challenged, improved, implemented, or adapted without requiring the author's software platform. It aims to make assumptions explicit, preserve provenance, encourage independent validation, and help different implementations exchange reproducible experiment definitions and results.
